# Economy and games

Every member has a wallet and a bank in each server. Coins earned in one server stay in that server.

## Getting started

- `!balance` shows your wallet and bank.
- `!daily` pays 500 coins and adds 50 more for every day in a row, up to a bonus of 1,000 (the streak keeps going if you claim again within 48 hours). It can be claimed about once a day.
- `!work` pays out once an hour, and `!weekly` and `!monthly` pay bigger amounts.
- `!deposit 500` moves coins to your bank, where they are safe from robbers, and `!withdraw 500` takes them back out.
- `!networth` adds up everything you own.

## Earning more

`!beg`, `!crime`, `!fish`, `!hunt` and `!mine` give coins or items. Fishing, hunting and mining need the matching tool from the shop (`!buy rod`, `!buy pickaxe`).

## The shop and inventory

- `!shop` lists items, `!buy padlock 2` buys, `!sell rod` sells back.
- `!inventory` shows what you own.
- A padlock stops the next `!rob` against you, then breaks.

## Risky games

| Game | Command |
|---|---|
| Coin flip (win to double) | `!bet` |
| Guess a die roll (pays 5 to 1) | `!dicebet` |
| Higher or lower, build a streak and cash out | `!highlow` |
| Cards | `!blackjack` |
| Casino | `!slots`, `!roulette`, `!scratch` (match symbols on a scratch card) |
| Steal | `!rob @user` (can fail and cost you coins) |
| Group bank job | `!heist` (others can join for 45 seconds) |
| Lottery | `!lottery buy 5` (100 coins a ticket, up to 50), `!lottery info`, drawn by staff with `!lottery draw` |

## Giving

`!give @user 100` and `!gift` move coins or items to someone else.

## Staff tools

`!addmoney @user 1000` adds coins (or removes them with a negative amount), for events and rewards. Coins are capped at one trillion per member.

## Good to know

- Nothing here costs real money. Coins have no value outside the server.
