# VIP plans

VIP is an optional upgrade for a single server. Everything in Risk works without it. VIP adds extra commands, scheduled announcements, embed-style auto-replies and a custom accent colour, and the higher plans add tools for running big communities.

## The plans

There are four plans. Prices and checkout links are on the [VIP page](/vip) of the main site.

- **Basic** is paid monthly and is the base. It unlocks scheduled announcements, embed auto-replies, an accent colour and the Basic commands.
- **VIP White** is paid monthly and includes everything in Basic, plus custom commands, auto reactions, a private welcome DM, the embed editor and a staff list.
- **VIP Supreme** is paid monthly and includes everything in White, plus power tools for large servers: auto delete, server-wide locking, backups, a member counter, and more custom commands.
- **Lifetime** is paid once and includes every feature of every plan, forever, plus the Lifetime-only commands. New VIP features are included.

## What every VIP server gets

- **Scheduled announcements.** Repeat a message in a channel on a timer, from the dashboard (Announcements tab) or with `!schedule #channel 6h Your message`. Up to 50 per server.
- **Embed-style auto-replies.** The dashboard's Auto-replies tab gets a "Send as an embed" switch.
- **Accent colour.** `!vipcolor #5865f2` or the dashboard VIP tab changes the colour of Risk's embeds in your server (welcome and level-up embeds, auto-reply embeds and more).
- **VIP badge and tab** in your server's dashboard.

## Buying and activating

1. Choose a plan on the VIP page and pay with the provider linked there.
2. Tell the team the server ID you want it on in the support server. Staff switch VIP on for that server, and it shows up in the dashboard's VIP tab and with `!vip`.
3. Monthly plans stay on until the end of the period you paid for.

`!vip` shows your server's plan, `!viptier` explains the tiers and `!vipcommands` lists which commands your plan unlocks.

## Good to know

- VIP belongs to a server, not a person. It follows the server, not whoever paid.
- If a VIP command says your server isn't on the right plan, `!vipcommands` shows what you are missing.
- The limits that matter: custom commands are capped at 50 on White and 200 on Supreme and Lifetime.

See every VIP command, with the plan that unlocks it, in [VIP commands](vip-commands).
