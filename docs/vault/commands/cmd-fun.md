# Fun commands

77 commands. Run `!help <command>` or see the examples below.

- **8ball**: Asks the magic 8-ball a question. Example: `will it rain`
- **advice**: Gives a piece of advice.
- **bite**: Bites someone. Example: `@user`
- **blush**: Blushes.
- **bonk**: Bonks someone. Example: `@user`
- **boot**: Kicks someone with a GIF. Example: `@user`
- **bubble**: Turns your message into bubble wrap, one pop per letter. Example: `pop me`
- **cat**: Shows a random cat picture.
- **catfact**: Tells a random cat fact.
- **choose**: Picks one option from a list split by | or commas. Example: `pizza | tacos | sushi`
- **clap**: Puts claps 👏 between 👏 words. Example: `nice work`
- **coinflip**: Flips a coin.
- **compliment**: Gives someone a nice compliment. Example: `@user`
- **connect4**: Plays Connect 4 against another member with buttons. Example: `@user`
- **cry**: Cries.
- **cuddle**: Cuddles someone. Example: `@user`
- **dadjoke**: Fetches a random dad joke.
- **dance**: Dances.
- **dare**: Gives a dare.
- **dog**: Shows a random dog picture.
- **duel**: Fights another member in a turn-based button duel. Example: `@user`
- **emojify**: Turns text into big emoji letters. Example: `hello`
- **fact**: Shares a random fact.
- **fakeban**: Pretends to ban someone (nobody is banned). Example: `@user`
- **fight**: Starts a silly fight with someone. Example: `@user`
- **fortune**: Reads your fortune.
- **fox**: Shows a random fox picture.
- **guess**: Guess a number from 1 to 100 in six tries.
- **hack**: Runs a pretend hacking animation (all fake, nothing happens). Example: `@user`
- **hangman**: Plays hangman: type single letters or the whole word in chat.
- **highfive**: High-fives someone. Example: `@user`
- **horoscope**: Reads today's horoscope for a zodiac sign. Example: `leo`
- **howcute**: Rates how cute someone is. Example: `@user`
- **hug**: Hugs someone. Example: `@user`
- **iq**: Reveals someone's completely made-up IQ. Example: `@user`
- **joke**: Tells a joke.
- **kiss**: Kisses someone. Example: `@user`
- **lenny**: Adds a lenny face to your message. Example: `nice`
- **luck**: Shows how lucky someone is today. Example: `@user`
- **mathrace**: Race to solve a math problem. First correct answer wins.
- **mock**: Turns text into mOcKiNg cAsE. Example: `you can't be serious`
- **neverhaveiever**: Posts a Never Have I Ever prompt.
- **number**: Picks a random number in a range. Example: `1 100`
- **pat**: Pats someone. Example: `@user`
- **pickup**: Sends a cheesy pickup line.
- **poke**: Pokes someone. Example: `@user`
- **punch**: Punches someone. Example: `@user`
- **quote**: Shares a short inspirational line.
- **rate**: Rates anything out of 10. Example: `pineapple pizza`
- **reactiontest**: Tests your reaction time with a button.
- **reverse**: Reverses your text. Example: `hello`
- **riddle**: Asks a riddle. First correct answer in 30 seconds wins.
- **roast**: Roasts someone, all in good fun. Example: `@user`
- **roll**: Rolls dice like 2d6+3, or a single number like 100. Example: `2d6+3`
- **rps**: Plays rock paper scissors against the bot. Example: `rock`
- **russianroulette**: Pulls the trigger in a harmless game of chance.
- **say** (needs Manage Messages): Makes the bot say something without pinging anyone. Example: `hello everyone`
- **scramble**: Unscramble a word. First correct answer in 30 seconds wins.
- **ship**: Checks how well two people match. Example: `@user @user`
- **shout**: Shouts your message in capital letters. Example: `hello everyone`
- **shrug**: Adds a shrug to your message. Example: `no idea`
- **slap**: Slaps someone with a wet noodle. Example: `@user`
- **spoiler**: Hides each word of your message behind a spoiler. Example: `the butler did it`
- **tableflip**: Flips a table. Example: `so annoying`
- **thisorthat**: Asks a this-or-that question with reactions.
- **tickle**: Tickles someone. Example: `@user`
- **tictactoe**: Starts a game of tic-tac-toe with someone. Example: `@user`
- **topic**: Starts a conversation with a random topic.
- **trivia**: Asks a multiple-choice trivia question.
- **truth**: Gives a truth question.
- **typing**: Race to type a sentence exactly. First to finish wins.
- **unflip**: Puts the table back. Example: `sorry`
- **uwu**: Turns text into uwu speak. Example: `hello there`
- **vibe**: Checks someone's vibe for today. Example: `@user`
- **wave**: Waves hello.
- **wheel**: Spins a wheel with your comma-separated options. Example: `pizza, sushi, tacos`
- **wyr**: Asks a would-you-rather question.
