# Economy commands

33 commands. Run `!help <command>` or see the examples below.

- **addmoney** (needs Administrator): Adds or removes coins from someone's wallet. Example: `@user 1000`
- **balance**: Shows a wallet and bank balance. Example: `@user`
- **beg**: Begs for spare change.
- **bet**: Bets on a coin flip. Win to double it. Example: `100 heads`
- **blackjack**: Plays blackjack against the dealer with buttons. Example: `500`
- **buy**: Buys an item from the shop. Example: `padlock 2`
- **crime**: Attempts a risky crime for big coins, or a fine.
- **daily**: Claims your daily coins. Keep a streak for a bonus.
- **deposit**: Moves coins from your wallet to your bank. Example: `500`
- **dicebet**: Bets on the number a die will land on. A correct guess pays 5 to 1. Example: `100 4`
- **fish**: Goes fishing with a rod.
- **gift**: Gives one of your shop items to another member. Example: `@user padlock 1`
- **give**: Gives some of your wallet coins to someone. Example: `@user 100`
- **heist**: Starts a bank heist that others can join for 45 seconds.
- **highlow**: Guesses higher or lower to build a streak, then cash out. Example: `200`
- **hunt**: Hunts animals with a rifle for coins.
- **inventory**: Shows the items someone owns. Example: `@user`
- **lottery**: Shows the lottery pot and your tickets. Actions: buy, info, draw.
- **lottery buy**: Buys lottery tickets for 100 coins each (up to 50 per person). Example: `5`
- **lottery draw** (needs Manage Server): Draws the lottery now (staff only).
- **lottery info**: Shows the pot, the next draw and the last winner.
- **mine**: Mines for ore with a pickaxe.
- **monthly**: Claims your monthly coins.
- **networth**: Shows wallet, bank and item value added together. Example: `@user`
- **rob**: Tries to steal coins from someone's wallet. Example: `@user`
- **roulette**: Bets on a roulette spin: red, black, even, odd, low, high or a number 0-36. Example: `500 red`
- **scratch**: Scratches a card with 3 hidden boxes. Match symbols to win. Example: `50`
- **sell**: Sells an item back for half its price. Example: `rod`
- **shop**: Lists items you can buy.
- **slots**: Spins the slot machine. Example: `100`
- **weekly**: Claims your weekly coins.
- **withdraw**: Moves coins from your bank to your wallet. Example: `500`
- **work**: Works a shift for coins once an hour.
