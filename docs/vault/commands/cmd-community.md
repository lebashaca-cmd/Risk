# Community commands

67 commands. Run `!help <command>` or see the examples below.

- **approve** (needs Manage Messages): Approves a suggestion, with an optional reason. Example: `12 great idea`
- **autorole**: Shows the role new members get automatically.
- **autorole off** (needs Manage Roles): Stops giving new members a role.
- **autorole set** (needs Manage Roles): Gives new members a role when they join. Example: `@Member`
- **autorole show**: Shows the role new members get automatically.
- **birthday**: Shows a member's birthday. Also: set, remove, list, next. Example: `@user`
- **birthday list**: Lists birthdays in this server by month.
- **birthday next**: Shows the next few birthdays.
- **birthday remove**: Removes your saved birthday.
- **birthday set**: Saves your birthday as month and day. Example: `05-17`
- **birthdaychannel** (needs Manage Server): Sets where birthday messages go, or turns them off. Example: `#birthdays`
- **counting**: Shows the counting game status. Actions: set, off, reset.
- **counting off** (needs Manage Server): Stops the counting game.
- **counting reset** (needs Manage Server): Resets the count to zero.
- **counting set** (needs Manage Server): Starts the counting game in a channel. Example: `#counting`
- **deny** (needs Manage Messages): Denies a suggestion, with an optional reason. Example: `12 not right now`
- **giveaway** (needs Manage Server): Starts a giveaway: duration, winners, prize. Also: start, drop, end, reroll, cancel, list, info, require. Example: `1h 2 Discord Nitro`
- **giveaway cancel** (needs Manage Server): Removes a giveaway without picking a winner. Example: `123456789012345678`
- **giveaway drop** (needs Manage Server): Starts a drop where the first person to press Enter wins. Example: `Free role`
- **giveaway end** (needs Manage Server): Ends a giveaway now and picks winners. Example: `123456789012345678`
- **giveaway info**: Shows a giveaway's entries and winners. Example: `123456789012345678`
- **giveaway list**: Lists the giveaways that are running.
- **giveaway require** (needs Manage Server): Limits a giveaway to members with a role, or removes the limit with off. Example: `123456789012345678 @Member`
- **giveaway reroll** (needs Manage Server): Picks new winners for a finished giveaway. Example: `123456789012345678`
- **giveaway start** (needs Manage Server): Starts a giveaway with a time limit. Example: `30m 1 Steam gift card`
- **note** (needs Manage Messages): Keeps private staff notes about members. Actions: add, list, remove. Example: `add @user trusted`
- **note add** (needs Manage Messages): Adds a staff note to a member. Example: `@user helped in the last event`
- **note list** (needs Manage Messages): Shows the staff notes for a member. Example: `@user`
- **note remove** (needs Manage Messages): Removes one staff note by its number. Example: `@user 2`
- **reactionrole**: Gives roles when members react to a message. Use add, remove or list. Example: `list`
- **reactionrole add** (needs Manage Roles): Adds a reaction role to a message in this channel. Example: `123456789012345678 🎮 @Gamer`
- **reactionrole list**: Lists the reaction roles in this server.
- **reactionrole remove** (needs Manage Roles): Removes a reaction role from a message. Example: `123456789012345678 🎮`
- **report**: Reports a member to the staff team. Example: `@user spamming links`
- **reportchannel** (needs Manage Server): Sets the staff channel for member reports, or turns reports off. Example: `#reports`
- **rules**: Shows this server's rules.
- **setrules** (needs Manage Server): Saves the server rules shown by the rules command, or reset to clear them. Example: `1. Be kind. 2. No spam.`
- **starboard**: Shows the starboard settings.
- **starboard off** (needs Manage Server): Turns the starboard off.
- **starboard set** (needs Manage Server): Chooses the starboard channel. Example: `#starboard`
- **starboard show**: Shows the starboard channel and star threshold.
- **starboard threshold** (needs Manage Server): Sets how many stars a message needs, from 1 to 25. Example: `5`
- **streamalerts** (needs Manage Server): Sets the channel for stream alerts, and optionally a role to ping. Example: `#streams @StreamPing (or !streamalerts off)`
- **streamalerts off** (needs Manage Server): Turns stream alerts off.
- **streamers**: Ranks members by total time streamed.
- **streaming**: Shows who is streaming right now.
- **streamrole** (needs Manage Server): Picks a role to ping when someone starts streaming (leave empty to stop pinging). Example: `@Streams`
- **streamstats**: Shows a member's stream count, total time and last stream. Example: `@user`
- **streamtest** (needs Manage Server): Posts a sample 'is live' and 'stopped' alert in the alert channel.
- **suggest**: Posts a suggestion for the server to vote on. Example: `add a music channel`
- **suggestchannel** (needs Manage Server): Sets the channel suggestions are posted in, or turns them off. Example: `#suggestions`
- **tag**: Shows a saved tag. Manage them with tag add, edit, remove, list and info. Example: `rules`
- **tag add**: Creates a tag. Example: `rules Be kind to each other`
- **tag edit**: Changes a tag's text. Example: `rules Be kind and have fun`
- **tag info**: Shows who made a tag and how often it's used. Example: `rules`
- **tag list**: Lists the tags in this server. Example: `2`
- **tag remove**: Deletes a tag. Example: `rules`
- **tags**: Lists the tags in this server.
- **tiktokadd** (needs Manage Server): Watches a TikTok account and posts when it goes live and ends. Example: `@name @StreamPing`
- **tiktoklist**: Lists the TikTok accounts this server watches.
- **tiktokremove** (needs Manage Server): Stops watching a TikTok account. Example: `@name`
- **tiktoktest** (needs Manage Server): Checks one TikTok account right now and says what Risk sees. Example: `@name`
- **twitchadd** (needs Manage Server): Watches a Twitch channel and posts when it goes live and offline. Example: `shroud @StreamPing`
- **twitchlist**: Lists the Twitch channels this server watches.
- **twitchremove** (needs Manage Server): Stops watching a Twitch channel. Example: `shroud`
- **twitchtest** (needs Manage Server): Checks one Twitch channel right now and shows what the alert looks like. Example: `shroud`
- **verifysetup** (needs Manage Roles): Posts a Verify button that gives a role to whoever presses it. Example: `#verify @Member`
